Case study

Fellow Products

Shopify store optimization and custom features: responsive landing pages, variants, gift pricing, and a stock check at checkout.

Visit fellowproducts.com

Context

The engagement is recorded as storefront optimization plus custom merchandising behavior: landing pages, variants, gift pricing, and a stock check at checkout.

Contribution

  • Responsive landing pages across screen sizes.
  • Page-speed optimization. The project notes describe the scores as top-tier.
  • Multi-option variants, including color and material.
  • Gift logic and dynamic pricing with Shopify Scripts.
  • A checkout extension for stock validation.

Technical approach

Shopify Liquid, JavaScript, and CSS/SCSS. Gift pricing used Shopify Scripts. Stock validation used a checkout extension.
